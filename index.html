Label guide Risk · valuation · metrics · watchlists
Risk

Low = diversified and established. Moderate = real business with cycle or valuation risk. High / Very High = more execution, cash-burn, volatility, or early-stage risk.

Valuation

Reasonable = easier to justify from current business quality. Overvalued = priced for a lot of optimism. Speculative = future-potential driven. Undervalued = appears cheap versus potential.

AI / Data Center Relevance

Shows whether the company can benefit from AI, cloud, chips, power, cooling, or data-center demand while quantum matures.
