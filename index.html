Label guide · risk, valuation, metrics

Risk

Low = diversified and established. Moderate = real business with cycle or valuation risk. High / Very High = more execution, cash-burn, volatility, or early-stage risk.

Valuation

Reasonable = easier to justify from current business quality. Overvalued = priced for a lot of optimism. Speculative = future-potential driven.

AI / Data Center Relevance

Shows whether the company may benefit from AI, cloud, chips, power, cooling, or data-center demand while quantum matures.

Pro Metrics

6M average, 6M return, price vs averages, volatility, drawdown, and relative strength vs QQQ. Market cap, volume, P/S, and revenue growth come from fundamentals.
